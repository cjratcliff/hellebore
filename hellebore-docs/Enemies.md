Interesting enemy design keeps it from being a classic boomer shooter.
- Boomer shooters are all about constant movement. In contrast, this game is about thinking and moving at the right time.
### Complex
#### Stalker
- Enemy with long stilt legs.
- Due to its height in can see down in to crops you're hiding in.
- May use a spotlight or a laser scanner. More engaging and shows the player where it's looking so you have a chance to evade it.
- Very cool when it goes right over you.
- Inspiration: Tallboys (Dishonored), Striders (HL2), Crow Fishers (Mad Max: Fury Road)
#### Ghost
- Ghost that disappears and is harmless in light.
- Can go through walls.
#### Dementor
- Enemy that makes nearby lights go out, like a dementor.
- They probably flicker first to give the player some warning and a chance to kill the creature.
- Maybe they will also not attack if there's too much light. So you're safe near a fire.
#### Crebain
- Flyers that can spot the player and alert other nearby creatures.
#### Scarab/rat swarm
- They don't attack you, they go through your position, dealing damage if they hit. 
- Possibly summoned by a sorcerer. 
- Can be dodged like a bullet hell but easier to get on top of something. 
- Alternatively a flying version where being in a field is safe rather than a hazard - easier to see without looking down.
#### Illusionist
- Summons one or two illusory clones of themselves so they can sneak up on you.
- The clones themselves are harmless and will disappear if you get too close.
- Shooting a clone doesn't harm it but you will see the illusion ripple.
#### Death eater
- You'll see them streaking across the sky as a cloud of black smoke.
- If they spot you below they'll come down to attack and may come down in front, behind or to your side, turning into their true form when on the ground and charge at your last position. 
- After being hit two or three times they turn back to smoke and go to recover somewhere nearby (still dangerous). Follow the smoke and hit them again to kill them.
### Simple
#### Flamer
- Heavy enemy with a flamethrower. Explodes if you shoot the fuel tanks or get a headshot but otherwise hard to kill.
#### Giant spiders
- About the size of a dog. When they get close enough they leap at the player.
#### Imps
- Hurls fireballs at the player. Agile.
#### Trash mob
- Melee attacker.
