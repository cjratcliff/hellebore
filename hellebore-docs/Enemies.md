Interesting enemy design keeps it from being a classic boomer shooter.
- Boomer shooters are all about constant movement. In contrast, this game is about thinking and moving at the right time.

- Enemy with long stilt legs.
	- Due to its height in can see down in to crops you're hiding in.
	- May use a spotlight or a laser. More engaging and shows the player where it's looking so you have a chance to evade it.
	- Very cool when it goes right over you.
	- Inspiration: Tallboys (Dishonored), Striders (HL2), Crow Fishers (Mad Max: Fury Road)
- Ghost that disappears and is harmless in light. Can go through walls.
- Trash mob. Melee attacker.
