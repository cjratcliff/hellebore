### Thoughts on Far Cry 4
- Things that are better in Far Cry 2:
	- Enemies having body armour is annoying. It turns enemies into bullet sponges since it's pretty much impossible to hit a moving target in the head. It makes sense with fully automatic weapons to keep it challenging, but with semi-automatic getting a hit is an achievement and you want it to mean something.
	- Mortar rounds suck. There's no counter-play. Better to find other ways to stop camping.
	- Helicopters suck. You can't hide from them, you die quickly and there's very little you can do if you run out of rockets.
	- Heavy enemies are a good idea but it's not fun to round a corner and get obliterated, unable to do anything. Especially since unlike in Far Cry 3, they're no longer vulnerable at the back of the head. Tagging is meant to prevent this but...
		- Tagging enemies breaks immersion. Better to use sound or other supernatural effects to indicate a powerful enemy is nearby.
	- Tagging is now automatic when you have someone in iron sights for long enough so you can't avoid it.
- Outpost maps need to be much bigger.
### Thoughts on Doom
- The player is robust enough that you don't die from a single mistake, which would be frustrating. But at the same time you don't have magically regenerating health so accumulated mistakes will kill you eventually.
